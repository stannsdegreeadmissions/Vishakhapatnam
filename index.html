Who We Are

A Premier Institution for Women's Education

St. Ann's College for Women, Malkapuram, Visakhapatnam, stands as a beacon of academic excellence and women's empowerment. Affiliated and autonomous, the college offers a wide spectrum of undergraduate programmes under AICTE guidelines, nurturing students to become confident, capable leaders of tomorrow.
